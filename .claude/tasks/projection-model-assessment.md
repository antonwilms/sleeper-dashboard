## A. Current-mechanism assessment

### A.1 The statistical form

The veteran path (`seasonProjection.js → computeNextSeasonProjection`) is a **log-linear model with hand-set coefficients**: `projectedPPG = basePPG × Π fᵢ`, i.e. `exp(log basePPG + Σ log fᵢ)` — a linear model in log space whose per-feature effect sizes were chosen by hand, applied to bucketed or percentile-transformed features. Three distinct normalization families produce the factors:

| Family | Factors | Transform |
|---|---|---|
| **Bucketed label lookups** | share trend (Step 3), regression (Step 4), momentum (Step 5), breakout/bounce-back/TD-reliance (5c), depth chart (Step 8), every rookie table | discretize a feature into labels → hand-set multiplier per label |
| **Cohort-percentile + shrinkage** | efficiency (5e, `efficiencyMetrics.js → computeEfficiencyFactor`), snap share (5f) and RZ own-rate (5g, `usageMetrics.js → computeUsageFactors`), team-RZ-share (5h, `teamRzShare.js → computeTeamRzShareFactor`) | percentile within position cohort, shrink toward neutral, map to a hand-set ± range (±10/6/5/5%) — an empirical-Bayes-flavored standardization with asserted output amplitude |
| **Clamped linear maps** | age delta (Step 2, clamp [0.80, 1.10]), trajectory (5d, `1 + slope × 0.35` clamp [0.93, 1.07]), team offense (Step 7, `(16 − rank)/200`), QB quality (7b, `(q − 50)/100 × 0.10`) | continuous feature → linear multiplier with hand-set slope + clamp |

Two hand-set **interaction terms** exist: the share-trend swing is dampened by share volatility (Step 3) and the regression swing by consistency (Step 4) — multiplicative dampeners on the deviation-from-1.0, not fitted interactions.

On top of the product sit: the **kNN comp blend** (Step 9, `compsIntegration.js → computeCompBlend` over `careerComps.js → findCareerComps`) — shrinkage of the pipeline estimate toward a nearest-neighbour career-arc estimate, with blend weight = `MAX_COMP_WEIGHT (0.35) × compConfidence (0.45·count + 0.40·similarity + 0.15·subsequent-seasons) × pipelineUncertainty ({low 1.0, medium 0.6, high 0.25})`; the **confidence label**, a qualifying-season-count tier (not a predictive interval); the **sanity rails** (`combinedNewFactor` clamp [0.67, 1.50], final clamp [0, 40]); and a separate **projectedGames** model (Step 6, weighted GP × injury-count multipliers × absence-shape refinement via `durabilitySignals.js → classifyInjurySeason`, clamp [8, 17]).

The **rookie path** (`seasonProjection.js → rookieProjection`) is a prior-based multiplicative table model: `baseline × ageMult × ktcMult × collegeContribution × nflDraftMultiplier`, product clamp [0.45, 1.85], fixed `projectedGames = 14`.

### A.2 Where hand-tuning is load-bearing

Every coefficient in the model is hand-set: the Step-1 recency weights (50/30/20), every bucket boundary and label multiplier, every percentile-family output range, both dampener scales, both clamp rails, the comp-blend weight structure, the entire rookie table set, and the Step-6 games multipliers. Two pieces of hand-tuning are *structurally* load-bearing rather than merely numeric:

1. **Independence-by-construction.** Factors multiply independently; nothing rebalances one factor against another. The whole stack is calibrated to a deliberately low total amplitude (measured `combinedNewFactorRaw` p5–p95 ≈ 0.82–1.135 over 2012–2025, n = 1,504 — `docs/projection.md` Step 5 combine note), which is what keeps 10 independent multipliers from stacking pathologically. `docs/projection.md` already documents the scaling ceiling: worst-case stack ≈ 1.46 vs the 1.50 rail, with the prescribed escalation at factor #13–14 being a "normalized additive-index restructure" — i.e., the current form's own docs anticipate that adding signals eventually forces a re-parameterization (relevant to §B).
2. **Position structure via gates, not weights.** QB is gated out of 5f/5h; RB/WR/TE differ in 5e metric sets and 5g/5h categories — but where a factor applies, its magnitude is position-uniform. Whether momentum matters as much for RBs as WRs is not a question the current form can express.

### A.3 Empirically grounded vs. asserted magnitudes

**Grounded (partial-β validated or measured):**
- **Team-RZ-share (5h)** — the only *active* factor with a controlled empirical basis: standardized partial β +0.20 RB / +0.17 WR/TE after controlling for own-rate, overall share, and snap share; monotone quintiles; own-rate β negative in the same model (`docs/projection.md` Step 5h; data repo `advstats-backtest.md` decision 8). Caveat: the validation grounds the signal's *existence and orthogonality*; the ±5% output range mirrors D2's convention and is itself asserted.
- **Validated demotions/exclusions** — aDOT capture-only (r = 0.289, volume-confounded — `docs/projection.md` §aDOT); `breakoutAgeFactor` demoted (weak once dominator + draft slot present); WOPR excluded as collinearity-inflated and `target_share` partial β ≈ 0 (advstats-backtest decisions 4/6; eval §E-1/2). Negative results are the best-grounded part of the system.
- **The clamp rails** — calibrated against the measured factor-product distribution (n = 1,504), not guessed.
- **Partially grounded** — the rookie NFL-draft-slot table ("derived from dynasty hit-rate analysis", `seasonProjection.js → resolveNflDraftFactor`), grounded in hit-rate data but not partial-β-controlled against the other rookie multipliers.

**Asserted (domain-reasoned, unfalsified):** everything else — all Step 2–5d magnitudes, the 5e–5g output ranges, Steps 6/7/7b/8, the comp-blend weights, the rookie age/KTC/college tables, the Step-1 recency weights. The project already treats mechanism changes to these as backtest-gated (e.g. the Step-7b rostered-only vs league-wide QB-map swap is explicitly held pending backtest — app CLAUDE.md pipeline note), so the discipline exists; what doesn't exist yet is a harness that can *fit or falsify* the asserted magnitudes jointly (§A.4).

### A.5 Dynasty-score consumption (scope-limited note)

`projectedPPG` does **not** feed the dynasty score — `dynastyScore.js → computeDynastyScore` is a parallel composite, and today's market-vs-model divergence (`computeMarketDivergence`) runs on dynasty score vs KTC, not on the projection. But the decision-engine design makes *projected production the input the dynasty value discounts forward* (`docs/dynasty-decision-engine-design.md` → Player Evaluation Framework), so the mechanism chosen here becomes the substrate of the future DCF-style value model. The mechanism should therefore be judged on three axes: Y+1 accuracy, decomposability (the delta narrative), and multi-year extensibility (chaining via the age-curve machinery already in Step 2). No other dynasty-score coupling is in scope.

## B. Ideal mechanism (unconstrained by current data)

### B.1 The four options

**(1) Keep the hand-tuned multiplicative stack.** Maximal transparency, zero data dependency, and its low-amplitude calibration embodies the research's humility (eval claim 10). But every magnitude is unfalsified (§A.3), position structure is gate-only, and the form has a documented scaling ceiling (§A.2). Keeping it forever means the model side of the wedge can never claim empirical grounding beyond one factor.

**(2) Fit the weights of the existing stack.** Re-parameterize as `projectedPPG = basePPG × Π fᵢ^{wᵢ}` — in log space, a regularized linear regression of `log(actualPPG) − log(basePPG)` on the `log fᵢ`, fit per position, with the hand-tuned stack as the shrinkage prior (ridge toward wᵢ = 1). Fitted exponents preserve the factor decomposition *exactly*: each factor still displays as one multiplier (`fᵢ^{wᵢ}`), the adjustment-narrative machinery is unchanged in kind, and a wᵢ ≈ 0 is an honest, legible verdict ("this factor is empirically dead") rather than a silent reweighting. 10–16 parameters per position is well within what a 2013–2025 pooled panel supports. This is also precisely the "normalized additive-index restructure" that `docs/projection.md` already names as the escalation path — fitting the weights and restructuring the index are the same move.

**(3) Move to a learned model (regularized regression / GBM on raw features).** On expected accuracy: the research's 50–70% explained-variance ceiling (eval claim 10) bounds what *any* form can gain; position-season cohorts are 30–80 players and pooled panels are a few thousand non-independent rows (advstats-backtest decision 7's caveat), where GBM variance eats most of its bias advantage. On the moat: a GBM's SHAP values are *attributions*, not a factor decomposition — they explain a prediction locally but cannot be narrated as "role growing, +4%; regression from outlier year, −6%" the way the product form can, and the decision-engine doc makes that narration the product's trust anchor ("The 'why' one tap from any value"; every competitor a black box). **Rejected as the shipped form.**

**(4) Hybrid: transparent factor layer + fitted head, with a learned challenger offline.** Option 2 *is* the fitted head over the transparent factor layer; the hybrid adds a GBM trained on the same panel as an **offline challenger only** — a diagnostic that (a) bounds how much accuracy the transparent form leaves on the table and (b) mines residual structure for *missing factors* (a persistent GBM edge concentrated in some cohort is a lead for a new transparent factor, not a reason to ship the GBM).

**Recommendation: Option 4 = ship Option 2, keep Option 3 offline.** Stated per the governing rule: **fitting/activating weights is scoring-affecting and backtest-gated (eval §E-3)** — the fitted weights ship only if they beat the hand-tuned stack out-of-sample under season-blocked validation (§E Phase 2). Cross-repo impact: none at fit time (offline analysis in the data repo); at activation, app-side `seasonProjection.js` weight constants + the snapshot `projection`/`factors` contract and `docs/signal-registry.md` reclassifications (see §F.5).

### B.2 Transparency-as-moat: the tradeoff argued, not assumed

The wedge's economic value is a *defensible* market-vs-model delta: a manager acts on "the model disagrees with KTC **because** X, Y, Z" and can audit each term. Strip the decomposition and the delta is indistinguishable from noise-vs-crowd — the product becomes one more opaque projection, competing on accuracy alone in a domain where the accuracy ceiling is low and shared. Quantitatively: with ≤50–70% of variance explainable at all, the realistic gap between a well-specified fitted log-linear model and a GBM on identical features is a few percent of RMSE; the moat cost of opacity is total. The asymmetry decides it. The one scenario that would reopen the question — the offline challenger showing a *large, persistent, cohort-concentrated* edge — is exactly the scenario where the right response is to identify and add the missing transparent factor.

### B.3 Uncertainty quantification under the 50–70% ceiling

The ceiling means intervals matter more than point-estimate polish. Laddered, per the decision-engine doc's "start with confidence tiers … upgrade to empirical distributions" and its no-fabricated-SDs rule:

1. **Now:** keep the tier labels (`high/medium/low/rookie`) — they are honest sample-size statements.
2. **Retrospective empirical bands:** residual quantiles (e.g. P25–P75) by position × confidence tier, computed from a reconstructed historical panel (reconstructable factors → pseudo-projections 2013 → 2025 outcomes). Display-layer only — **scoring-decoupled by construction**, no backtest gate needed to *show* honest bands.
3. **Forward recalibration:** once in-basis grading produces true snapshot residuals (2027+), recalibrate on those — the honest version, since forward residuals include the ephemeral-input error (depth staleness, offseason team churn) that any retrospective panel structurally understates.
4. **Archetype-conditional bands** (age × role-stability × injury history) once forward sample permits.

The comp blend already emits a free ensemble-disagreement signal — `|compPPG − pipelinePPG|` is recorded in `factors` — worth including as a band-width feature in step 2.

### B.4 Position-specific structure

Separate fitted weight vectors per position: QB / RB / WR / TE, with WR+TE pooled as a fallback where TE n is thin (mirroring advstats-backtest decision 6's per-position discipline). The existing gates stay (they encode structural zeroes — QB snap share carries no information); fitting makes everything else empirical. Position-specific *feature* upgrades, each backtest-gated: QB — EPA/attempt and CPOE as candidates against passer rating inside the Step-5e efficiency composite (eval D-1; feasibility §D.1); WR/TE — air-yards-share level as the first joint-model candidate (eval D-4); RB — carry-share level and team-context (PROE) interaction candidates (§C-3, §C-7).

### B.5 Rookie and veteran paths under the chosen form

- **Veteran:** fitted exponents per B.1(2); comp blend retained as the shrinkage layer, with its weight becoming one more fittable coefficient in a later stage (the current confidence-weighted α-blend is the hand-set prior).
- **Rookie:** keep the prior-based multiplicative table model *as is*. Draft-class n is dozens; fitting per-multiplier exponents would fit noise. The right empirical treatment is whole-path **calibration grading** (realized rookie PPG vs projected, by draft tier) once graded rookie cohorts accrue from snapshots — a Phase-3 activity. No new rookie mechanism proposed.
- **Multi-year:** no separate multi-horizon model. The Y+1 engine stays the base; multi-year production streams for the future DCF consumer are chained Y+1 projections advanced along the empirical age curves (Step 2 machinery) — decomposability survives chaining, which a direct multi-year learned model would not offer.

## E. Recommendation + phased path

**Headline: adopt the hybrid (ship fitted per-position exponents over the existing transparent factor stack, hand-tuned values as the shrinkage prior; GBM strictly as an offline challenger). Do it in the order below, and let nothing scoring-affecting activate without clearing the joint-model backtest — eval §E-3 governs every item marked ⚑.**
